Sleep Channel Private Reviews

A private tool for reviewing synthetic narration before any production decision.

What the app does

The account owner uses this tool to place narration recordings in a dedicated, owner-only Google Drive folder, compare revisions and check the integrity of stored files. The app checks account ownership and file permissions before returning a review link to its owner.

This website explains the tool. It does not host recordings, collect Google credentials or provide a sign-in service. The tool is for its operator's own account; public registrations are not offered.

Google Drive access

The app requests only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This permits access to files created by the app or specifically made available to it, rather than unrestricted access to the owner's entire Drive.

It uses file content and metadata to upload recordings, confirm ownership and Restricted sharing, check storage limits and verify checksums. It does not create public sharing permissions. A review URL does not give another person access to the recording.

Owner control

The owner decides what to review and retains control of recordings and Google authorization. Private review does not authorize public distribution or a YouTube upload.

Read the privacy policy for data access, storage, retention and deletion details, and the terms of use for this personal tool.

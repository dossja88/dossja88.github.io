Sleep Channel Private Reviews

Privacy policy

Prepared October 18, 2026. This policy describes Sleep Channel Private Reviews, a personal, single-owner narration review tool.

Information accessed and why

The app uses the Google Drive drive.file permission to access recordings and related review files it creates or that the owner specifically makes available to it. It reads and writes those files for private review and integrity verification. It reads file names, identifiers, parent folders, ownership, permissions, sizes and checksum information. It also checks the account's permission identifier and available storage to avoid using the wrong account or exceeding review storage limits.

During initial authorization, the setup process checks the Google account email against the intended owner's account. It does not request Gmail access, contacts, unrestricted Drive access or YouTube access.

Storage, access and sharing

Recordings and recovery manifests are stored in the owner's Google Drive with owner-only access. The private execution environment also processes recording bytes and stores temporary working copies and protected transfer records outside the source repository. Recovery metadata includes file identifiers, checksums and review configuration.

OAuth credentials are held in protected configuration for the execution environment. Access tokens are used in memory when calling Google APIs. OAuth credentials, recordings and upload-session addresses are not included in the public website or public source files.

The tool sends authorized requests to Google and processes files in its execution environment. These service providers operate under their own terms and privacy policies. App operators do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or send Drive-derived data to train AI models. The app does not grant public, domain-wide, group or additional-user sharing. The owner can independently change or remove their own files in Google Drive.

Use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention, deletion and revocation

Review files and recovery records remain until the owner decides to remove them. There is no automatic deletion schedule. Working copies can remain in the private execution workspace until the owner or workspace lifecycle removes them; deletion of a Drive file does not automatically erase separate local copies or recovery metadata.

The owner may revoke the app's access through Google Account settings and may delete recordings or manifests in Google Drive. Revocation stops future authorized API access but does not itself delete existing files. To remove retained local copies, transfer state or stored authorization, the owner must also arrange their removal from the execution environment and secure settings. The app has no public signup or public deletion-request portal.

This informational website

These static pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising, sign-in form or tracking cookies set by this site. The hosting provider may process connection information, including IP addresses, for delivery and security under its own privacy policy. If hosted on GitHub Pages, see the GitHub privacy statement.

Contact and changes

This tool is operated for its owner's own account. For authorization-related questions, use the user-support contact shown on the app's Google consent screen. No public customer service or external user accounts are offered.

The operator will update this policy before changing the tool's data practices or offering it to other users.
